Converter residual and combined modelling effect per reference tool, one marker per grid, the black bar the median. Converter residual: pgml against the tool with every modelling option matched to the tool, which is what the converter itself leaves. All at pgml defaults: the same grid with every option at its pgml default, which is what a user gets without a preset. Sum of isolated: the sum of the single-option effects. Interaction: combined minus that sum, computed on the complex voltages; a large value means several options act on the same quantity. Fundamental panels: the largest difference of any complex node voltage at the fundamental, in per unit. Harmonic panel: the largest difference of any complex harmonic node voltage, relative to the largest voltage of that order, over the orders above one. An effect of exactly zero is drawn at 1e-10. Medians: pandapower fundamental residual 4.1e-10, combined 8.4e-7; power-grid-model fundamental residual 6.7e-3, combined 5.7e-3; OpenDSS fundamental residual 2.4e-2, combined 2.5e-4; OpenDSS harmonic residual 1.0e0, combined 0.
