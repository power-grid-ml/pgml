Whole harmonic studies per second against the batch, one panel per grid (IEEE 33-bus, Kerber Vorstadtnetz, Kerber Vorstadtnetz x4), thirteen orders in complex128. The solid pgml curves build every device's harmonic shunt from the power it draws in that scenario, which is the model OpenDSS solves and makes the per-order admittance one matrix per scenario; the dashed ones build it from the stored power, which shares one factorization per order across the batch and is a different model. At the largest batch each reached on the accelerator, sharing is faster by 6 times, 44 times and 108 times from left to right, and moves the agreement with OpenDSS on the leftmost grid from 3.2e-05 to 1.6e-04 per unit.
